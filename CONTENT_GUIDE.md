# Flenzo Culture content guide

## Replace images
All major image placeholders are organized near the top of `src/App.jsx`.

- `heroImage`: homepage hero
- `products[].images`: product gallery. Use one object for one photo, or multiple objects for Front/Back/Detail.
- `cultureStories`: The Culture / South Coast photography
- `communityStories`: Skate 4 the Ocean, Cup of Altinha, and beach cleanup photography
- `oceanImage`: Impact / Ocean background
